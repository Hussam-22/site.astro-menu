# Astro-Menu Website

Welcome to the Astro-Menu website! This platform is designed to revolutionize the way restaurants and food businesses manage their menus and orders. With a focus on digital solutions, Astro-Menu offers a seamless and intuitive experience for both customers and restaurant owners.

## Key Features

- **Dynamic QR Code Menu**: Create and update your menu in real-time. Customers can scan the QR code to view the latest menu.
- **Self Order**: Allow customers to place orders directly from their phones, reducing wait times and increasing efficiency.
- **Multi-Language Support**: Automatically translate your menu into multiple languages to cater to a diverse customer base.
- **Cloud-Based Dashboard**: Manage orders, track performance, and update menus from any device with internet access.
- **Analytics**: Gain insights into customer preferences and order trends to optimize your menu and operations.
- **Eco-Friendly**: Reduce paper waste by switching to digital menus.

## Getting Started

To get started with Astro-Menu, follow these steps:

1. **Sign Up**: Create an account on the Astro-Menu website.
2. **Set Up Your Menu**: Add meals, descriptions, and images to your digital menu.
3. **Generate QR Codes**: Print and display QR codes for customers to scan.
4. **Manage Orders**: Use the cloud-based dashboard to track and manage orders efficiently.
