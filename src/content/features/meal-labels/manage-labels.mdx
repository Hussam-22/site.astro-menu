You can either disable or delete meal labels.

- **Disable a Meal Label**: Disabling a label will hide it from the customer's menu and prevent them from filtering meals by that label. It also prevents admins from selecting the label when adding new meals.
- **Delete a Meal Label**: Deleting the label will completely remove it from the system.

Currently, Astro-Menu does not offer an option to manually edit translations for meal labels. However, this feature is in our future plans.
