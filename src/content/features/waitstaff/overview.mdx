In Astro-Menu, **staff** refers to the access links for your POS/Dashboard. You can think of staff as waitstaff and kitchen staff or as devices such as *Device 1*, *Device 2*, etc.

<Image
	src={WaitStaffListImage}
	alt="Admin dashboard meals list"
	class="w-fit rounded-xl sm:h-2/3 sm:w-2/3"
/>

When creating staff profiles, each will have a unique access link tied to its corresponding branch. The type of staff determines their dashboard access:

- **Waitstaff**: 
<br /> These users will have access to a dashboard where they can take customer orders, mark meals as unavailable, send orders to the kitchen staff, and handle payments.

- **Chef/Kitchen Staff**: 
<br /> These users will have access to a dashboard that allows them to view and prepare incoming orders. They can also mark meals as unavailable.

**Note**: You can assign staff to a branch either from the <FeatureLink text='Staff' link='/features/waitstaff/manage' /> page or directly within the <FeatureLink text='Branch waitstaff' link='/features/branches/waitstaff' /> page.
