From the **menu management** page, you can perform several key actions to customize and build your menu:

- **Rename Menu**: Change the menu name to improve organization and make it easier to identify.

- **Most Ordered Meals**:

  - Specify the number of most-ordered meals to display at the top of the meals and sections list. This feature highlights popular dishes for customers, improving their browsing experience.
  - Setting the value to **zero** will hide the **Most Ordered Meals** section, while the maximum allowed value is **10**.
  - The **Most Ordered Meals** section is dynamically generated by Astro-Menu, displaying items based on how frequently they have been ordered.

- **Delete Menu**:
  - When deleting a menu, the system will prompt you to choose a **Revert Menu**. This ensures that your <FeatureLink link='/docs/branches/tables-qr' style='dotted' text='table QRs'/> and <FeatureLink link='/docs/branches/menu-qr' text='social QR'/> codes automatically switch to the selected menu after the deletion is complete.

### Building your menu

To start building your menu, head to the <FeatureLink link='/docs/menus/meals-and-sections' style='dotted' text='Meals & Sections'/> tab where you will be able to create sections, add meals and edit prices.
