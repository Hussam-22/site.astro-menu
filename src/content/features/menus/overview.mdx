The **Menus** page is where you can design and organize your meals into various sections. Each section displays a specific group of meals, and each menu allows you to set different prices for the same meal. This flexibility enables you to quickly switch between different menus for your customers without the hassle of removing and updating meals and prices entirely.

You only need to provide menu name when you add it from the menus page.

<Image
	src={MenusListImage}
	alt="Admin dashboard meals list"

/>

This feature is especially valuable for situations like food trucks or mobile kitchens, where you might offer different sets of meals and prices depending on the location. With this capability, you can prepare full menus in advance and seamlessly swap them as needed.
