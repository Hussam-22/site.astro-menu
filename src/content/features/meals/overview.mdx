Managing your restaurant’s menu efficiently is crucial to providing a seamless dining experience for your customers. Astro-Menu admin dashboard equips you with robust tools for meal management, ensuring your menu is always current and well-organized

<Image src={MealsListImage} alt='admin dashboard meals list' class='rounded-2xl' />

One of key features of Astro-Menu is meals labeling/tagging and auto translations.

Use the <FeatureLink link='/features/meal-labels/overview' text='Meal Labels' /> feature to tag meals with attributes such as decaf, sugar-free, gluten-free, and more.

Astro-Menu will automatically translates your meal to the languages you have selected in your <FeatureLink link='/features/translation-settings' text='translation settings' />.

After adding your meals, you can start building your <FeatureLink link='/features/menus/manage-menu' text='menu' /> and use on your <FeatureLink link='/features/branches/tables-qr' text='tables QR' />.
