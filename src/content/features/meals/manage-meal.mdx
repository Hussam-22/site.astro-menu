Managing meals is straightforward. Similar to adding a new meal, you can update existing meals with enhanced control through additional settings.

### Key Considerations When Updating a Meal

- **Portion Update**: If a meal is already included in one of your <FeatureLink link='/features/menus/overview' text='menus' />, updating the portion names or prices will not automatically reflect in the menu. This is because a copy of the meal, including its details, is saved to the menu. To apply updates, you need to remove the meal from the menu and re-add it with the updated information.

- **Disabling Meals**: Disabling a meal will remove it from all <FeatureLink link='/features/menus/overview' text='menus' /> in which it appears. This provides a quick and efficient way to hide a meal across all menus without the need for manual removal. You can re-enable the meal at any time to make it visible again.

- **Translation Limitations**: When a meal is disabled, you cannot update its translation from the <FeatureLink link='/features/meals/translation' text='translation' /> tab.

## Settings

### Show "New" Label

You can add a **New** label at the top of the meal image to highlight that the meal is newly added to your QR Menu.

### Meal Status

Disabling a meal will remove it from all menus in which it appears, providing a streamlined way to hide it across the board. You can re-enable the meal at any time to restore its visibility.

### Delete Meal

Deleting a meal will completely remove it from the system, along with all its associated statistics. This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

## Translation

You can edit meal translations through the <FeatureLink link='/features/meals/translation' text='translation' /> tab.
