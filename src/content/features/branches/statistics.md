Gain valuable insights and better understand your restaurant's performance. Astro-Menu offers various metrics to help you dive deeper into your operations, including total orders, table turnover rates, average table turnover time, total income, average income per order, total scans, the top 10 most ordered meals, and staff performance.

You can adjust the time period to compare data with previous months for more comprehensive analysis.
