Astro-Menu enables you to search for completed or canceled orders, allowing you to review details, print orders, or resend invoices to customers.

You have three main methods to search for orders:

- **Search Multiple Orders (Quick Search)**

  - You can search for orders from the last 1 hour, 8 hours, or 24 hours. This type of search is useful for quickly reviewing recent orders, such as those placed within the past hour.

- **Search by Date**

  - This option allows you to find orders from a specific date, making it useful when you need to review all orders placed on a particular day.

- **Search for a Single Order**

  - This option lets you search for an order using its order number, ideal for when you need to view the details of a specific order.
