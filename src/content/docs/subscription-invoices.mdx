From the **Subscriptions and Invoices** page, you can view your plan details, subscription status, and subscription period. You can also access the Subscription Portal to manage your subscription. This includes upgrading or downgrading your plan, canceling your subscription, updating your payment information, and viewing or downloading invoices.
