In the **Staff Management** page, you have several options to manage your staff:

- **Change Staff Type**  
  Switch between _Chef_ or _Waitstaff_ based on the role of the staff/device.
- **Change Staff Name**  
  Edit the name of the staff member if necessary.

- **Assign Staff to a Branch**  
  Assign staff members to specific branches to define their access.

- **Change Staff Status**  
  Update the staff member's status to active or disabled.

- **Reset Staff Passcode**  
  Reset the passcode for a staff member if they forget it.

- **Delete Staff**  
  Remove a staff member from the system.

<TipCard text="Resetting the staff passcode or changing the staff status to 'disabled' will automatically sign out the staff from the dashboard." />

**Note**: For added security, staff members have 5 attempts before their account is locked. If the account is locked, it must be unlocked from the _Staff Management_ page.
