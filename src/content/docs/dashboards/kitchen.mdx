Astro-Menu provides your kitchen staff with an intuitive and easy-to-use dashboard. The Kitchen Dashboard is designed to help staff efficiently manage orders, track their status, and ensure timely meal preparation.

Whenever a new order is placed, it instantly appears on the Kitchen Dashboard in real-time. A bell sound alerts the staff for each new order, allowing them to promptly view the details and update the order status as "Completed" once the meal is ready to serve.

<TipCard
	text={`If your operations don't involve a kitchen, such as when waitstaff handle both order-taking and preparation, you can enable the "Skip Kitchen" feature from the Branch Settings page.`}
/>{' '}

### Key Features of the Kitchen Dashboard

1. **Real-Time Order Management**:  
   View all incoming orders instantly as they are placed.

2. **Order Status Updates**:  
   Mark orders as completed once the meal is ready to serve.

3. **Detailed Order Information**:  
   Access to details such as table numbers, order items, and the name of the waitstaff managing the order.

Each kitchen team member or kitchen device has a unique access URL to ensure secure and streamlined operations. You can retrieve these URLs for your staff from the <FeatureLink link='/docs/branches/waitstaff' text={`Branch's Staff Access Link Tab`} />.
