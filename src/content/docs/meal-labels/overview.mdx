Astro-Menu aims to enhance table turnover by providing customers with the option to filter meals based on dietary preferences, such as decaf, sugar-free, gluten-free, or by specific cravings like beef, chicken, seafood, and more.

Meal labels play a crucial role in improving table turnover. Therefore, it’s essential to add meal labels to your <FeatureLink link='/docs/meals/add-meal' text='meals' /> when adding them to the system for the first time.

### Adding Meal Labels

To add a meal label, click **Add** on the meal labels page and include labels that match your meals, such as beef, chicken, healthy, vegetarian, etc.

Astro-Menu will automatically translate your meal labels into the languages configured in your <FeatureLink link='/docs/translation-settings' text='translation settings' />.
