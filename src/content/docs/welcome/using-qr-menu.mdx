Here are a few questions and answers to help you learn more about using the QR Menu:

### Where I can find my QR Codes?

You can find your QR Codes under your branch. Simply click on your branch, and you'll see tabs at the top for <FeatureLink link='/docs/branches/social-qr' style='dotted' text='Social QR'/> and <FeatureLink link='/docs/branches/tables-qr' text='Tables QR' />.

You can easily download the <FeatureLink link='/docs/branches/tables-qr' text='Tables QR' /> and place them on your tables for customer use.

### Can customers pay directly from the menu?

Astro-Menu was designed with the principle of not profiting from your orders. Many customers still prefer to pay using their cards or cash, and they may dislike being pressured or tricked into leaving tips. Therefore, Astro-Menu focuses on managing the menu while leaving payment handling to you.

However, we don’t leave you unsupported. Astro-Menu guides customers to an order summary page where they can signal a waiter when they're ready to pay and optionally provide their email to receive an order invoice.

### How translation works?

Astro-Menu takes care of translation for you. When you add or update a meal name or description, the system automatically translates it into all the languages you have selected in the <FeatureLink link='/docs/translation-settings' style='dotted' text='translation settings'/> page.

### How to setup my menu ?

When you register with Astro-Menu, your account includes predefined settings and meals to help you get started quickly. However, if you'd prefer to build your setup from scratch, we recommend following these steps:

1. **Add <FeatureLink link='/docs/meal-labels/manage-labels' style='dotted' text='Meal Labels'/>**
   - Meal labels assist customers in filtering meals efficiently, which is crucial for improving table turnover.

2. **Add <FeatureLink link='/docs/meals/manage-meal' style='dotted' text='Meals'/>**
   - Begin by adding meals to the system, including the meal name, description, image, portion sizes, and associated labels.

3. **Add <FeatureLink link='/docs/menus/manage-menu' style='dotted' text='Menus'/>**
   - Create multiple menus, each featuring different sets of meals, sections, and meal prices.

4. **Add <FeatureLink link='/docs/branches/manage-branch' style='dotted' text='Branch'/>**
   - The branch acts as the central hub for managing your menus. Each branch can have distinct configurations such as the default menu, default language, tax value, Wi-Fi password, and more.
   - Be sure to set a <span class='text-black font-semibold'>default menu</span> for your branch so that your <FeatureLink link='/docs/branches/tables-qr' text='Tables QR'/> will reference it correctly.
