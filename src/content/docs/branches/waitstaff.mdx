Each branch has its own waitstaff and kitchen staff, each with a unique access link URL that allows them to log in to their PoS/Dashboard.

<TipCard text="You may refer to waitstaff and kitchen staff as devices, such as iPad 1 and iPad 2, instead of using staff names." />

You can create waitstaff accounts from the <FeatureLink link='/docs/waitstaff/manage' text='Waitstaff' /> page.

There are two types of <FeatureLink link='/docs/waitstaff/manage' text='Waitstaff' />: **staff** and **chef**.

- **Staff** are your waitstaff who take orders and send them to the kitchen.
- **Chef** refers to your kitchen staff who receive orders and notify the waitstaff when meals are ready to be served.

<TipCard text="You can enable *Skip Kitchen* from the branch management settings if you want your waitstaff to manage the entire order process or if the waitstaff themselves will be preparing the orders." />

You may change access code (password), disable access, and revoke access (force logout) for staff from this tab as well.
